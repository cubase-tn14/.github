## Production Overview
Cubase, developed by Steinberg, is one of the longest-standing and most respected digital audio workstations in professional music. It offers a mature, deeply featured environment for recording live instruments, sequencing MIDI, and mixing at studio quality. Over decades of refinement, Cubase has become a standard in composition and post-production, valued for reliability and precision.

The MIDI capabilities of Cubase are especially celebrated. Its Key Editor, Score Editor, and expression maps give composers granular control over performances, articulations, and notation. This makes Cubase a top choice for orchestral writing and film scoring, where nuanced MIDI programming and tight synchronization to picture are essential to a convincing mockup.

On the audio side, Cubase delivers a powerful mixer modeled on large-format consoles, complete with channel strips, VST effects, and advanced routing. Tools like VariAudio for pitch correction, Chord Track for harmony, and a comprehensive plugin suite mean Cubase handles every stage from tracking to final master within a single, cohesive workspace.

## Getting Started Playbook
Install Cubase, set up your audio interface in the Studio Setup panel, and create a new project. Add audio or instrument tracks, and Cubase is ready to record live input or receive MIDI from a keyboard within minutes.

Lay down your parts, then refine them in the Key Editor for MIDI or the sample editor for audio. Use the Cubase mixer to add effects and balance levels, and when the arrangement is complete, export a stereo mix or stems directly from Cubase.
